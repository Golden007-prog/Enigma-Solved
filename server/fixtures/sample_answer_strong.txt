In my internship at a logistics startup, our order-tracking API was taking 900 milliseconds per request, and I was asked to bring it under 200. I profiled the endpoint, found we were hitting PostgreSQL for the same shipment data on every call, and I added a Redis cache with a 30-second TTL keyed by shipment ID. After that, p95 latency dropped to 140 milliseconds and the timeout alerts stopped.
